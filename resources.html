Everything you need to get started

Guides for students and staff who want to organize or attend events.

Guidance below is sample content. Confirm official procedures with the university.

Resource categories

Venue booking

  1. Choose a venue (auditorium, lawn, sports ground).
  2. Check the date is free.
  3. Submit your request with your event proposal.
  4. Wait for confirmation.

Planning checklist: budget, team roles, equipment, promotion and arrangements.

Event rules

  • Every event needs an approved proposal.
  • A faculty advisor must be named.
  • Events must end by the agreed time.
  • Organizers are responsible for the venue after use.

Frequently asked questions

Who can organize an event?

Registered student societies with a faculty advisor.

How early should I submit a proposal?

As early as possible. Use the proposal form.

How do I register for an event?

Follow the registration link in the event's announcement on the News page.

Will I get a certificate?

Certificates depend on the organizer. Check the event details.
